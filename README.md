## My last three projects, and their core principles

I've been working on some projects for the past couple of months (some more than others) and here are some of them. You can try all of them on my website and learn more about them. 

<br>

### [Cue](https://prajjwalveerbasnet.com/projects/cue) &nbsp;<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/launching-d29922?style=flat-square&labelColor=0d1117" alt="launching">

**You cannot press a button at the instant of a sound.** Reaction time puts every hand-made
lyric timing around 150 ms late. Cue's goal is to tap *approximately* right, and let three independent
mechanisms improve the average human timing.

<a href="https://prajjwalveerbasnet.com/projects/cue#see-it-working">
  <img src="./images/readme/cue.gif" alt="Cue: tapping word timings over a real mix, then snapping them backwards onto detected onsets" width="1024">
</a>

> Tap space as each word ends, then turn snapping on. Real FFT, real adaptive threshold, real
> peak picking — decoded in the page, and the audio never leaves it.
> **[Try it →](https://prajjwalveerbasnet.com/projects/cue#see-it-working)**

<br>

### [Slipstream](https://prajjwalveerbasnet.com/projects/slipstream) &nbsp;<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/launching-d29922?style=flat-square&labelColor=0d1117" alt="launching">

**F1 usually focuses on the first five contenders. SlipStream lets you choose who you want to see.** It also provides everyone who has
raced since the 1950s their full history, along with customizable widgets.

<a href="https://prajjwalveerbasnet.com/projects/slipstream#see-it-working">
  <img src="./images/readme/slipstream.gif" alt="Slipstream: scrubbing the closing laps of the 2021 Abu Dhabi Grand Prix from official timing data" width="1024">
</a>

> Abu Dhabi 2021, lap 53 of 58, from official F1 timing data. Watch the safety car arrive.
> **[Replay it →](https://prajjwalveerbasnet.com/projects/slipstream#see-it-working)**

<br>

### [Shobha's Delicacies](https://prajjwalveerbasnet.com/projects/shobhas-delicacies) &nbsp;<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/shipped-3fb950?style=flat-square&labelColor=0d1117" alt="shipped">

**My mother runs a frozen-food kitchen in Kathmandu.** Like a small scale business it ran on a simple bill book and the notes app
on my phone. This app was built to replace both and make it scalable in the future with an inventory management system, POS and a storefront all running on one database, which I and five of our staff use daily.

<a href="https://prajjwalveerbasnet.com/projects/shobhas-delicacies">
  <img src="./images/readme/shobha.gif" alt="Two shelves, same deliveries, same orders: FIFO rotation wastes nothing while LIFO throws five batches away" width="1024">
</a>

> Two shelves, the same week, the same orders. The only difference is which end a hand
> reaches for, and which one ends up as waste.
> **[Run the week →](https://prajjwalveerbasnet.com/projects/shobhas-delicacies)**
